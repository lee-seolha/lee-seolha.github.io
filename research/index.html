Suspension, Stasis, Stagnation in British Romantic Literature

My dissertation project looks at various forms of suspension, stasis, and stagnation manifested during the period and different ways in which they were figured in Romantic literature through the works of William Wordsworth, Dorothy Wordsworth and John Clare. In investigating those terms, my dissertation suggests stagnation, which hasn’t received much attention in Romanticism as a useful word to describe Romantic imagination enabled by certain physical conditions.
